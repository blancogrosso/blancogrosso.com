ARQUITECTURA DE PROMPT PROMPT ARCHITECTURE

Prompt vs Resultado Prompt vs Result

anzu_raw.prompt

Ultra-realistic cinematic sports editorial photo of [PLAYER: adult male] portrayed as a field herpetologist / conservation biologist, premium campaign realism, serious attitude. Match the provided face reference for identity (high similarity). The provided club jersey must be visible under wardrobe (open lab coat/tunic, tasteful). Wardrobe (mandatory): white lab coat/tunic (slightly open), black nitrile gloves, clear laboratory safety glasses... Output: 3:4 vertical, 4K, extremely detailed, magazine cover realism. No text, no logos, no badges, no watermarks, no UI.

pepo_raw.prompt

Ultra-realistic cinematic sports editorial portrait of [PLAYER: adult male] sitting on the exact provided chair (same model, shape, armrests), arms wide open on the armrests, relaxed dominance posture. POV: drone camera hovering above and slightly in front of the subject (high angle top-down / 3-4 meters above, looking down), subject looks up directly toward the drone POV (eyes and head angled upward to camera). Match the provided face reference for identity and facial structure (high similarity)... 4K, extremely detailed, magazine cover vibe.
